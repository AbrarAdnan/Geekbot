Lantern staff FAQ

Answers to the questions people ask most about Lantern, the private assistant at Brightwater Studio. If your question is not here, ask Daniel Reyes.

A reminder first

Everything you type is sorted into one of three data classes: green, amber or red. Amber is client work in progress. Red is contracts, personal data and money. Know which one you are holding before you paste it anywhere.

Getting started

How do I get an account?

Go to one of the 45 minute training sessions. When you have attended, Daniel Reyes creates your account, usually within one working day. You get your own sign-in; never share it.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, Word, Markdown and plain text files, up to 50 MB each. Spreadsheets and pictures are not read.

What I may put in

Can I use Lantern for a client's contract?

Not on your own. Contracts are red data. Ask Tomasz Lewandowski for approval in writing first.

Can I paste a client's draft layout notes?

Yes. Work in progress is amber, and Lantern is allowed to handle amber data. Check the workspace name at the top of the chat before you paste, so the notes go into the right project.

May I use an outside AI website instead?

Only for green material, such as text that is already public. Never for amber or red data. If you did it by accident, tell Tomasz straight away.

Your conversations

How long does Lantern remember my chats?

Chat history stays for 90 days and is then deleted automatically at night. Documents you uploaded stay until the project is closed and a further 12 months have passed.

Can anyone read my chats?

The two administrators can see who used Lantern and when, but not what was written. They open chat logs only if Tomasz starts an incident.

When something goes wrong

Lantern is slow. What should I do?

Wait a minute: up to three people can chat at once, and a fourth waits in a queue. If it is still slow, tell Priya Nair, who is on call on Mondays and Thursdays.

I pasted something in the wrong place. Now what?

Tell Tomasz Lewandowski within 24 hours. If it was red data, phone him within one hour.

Can I send Lantern's answer straight to a client?

No. A person must read and check every answer before it leaves the studio. Lantern drafts; you are responsible for what is sent.
