10 March 2025: Policy draft
Present: Maya Okafor, Daniel Reyes, Tomasz Lewandowski, Hannah Frost.

Discussion
- Tomasz proposed keeping chat logs for 30 days, to keep the amount of stored client material small.
- Hannah said designers go back to a client thread up to three months later, for example when a client returns after a pause, and a 30 day limit would lose that context.
- Maya agreed with Hannah.

Decisions
- Chat logs are kept for 90 days, not 30.
- Audit logs, which hold no conversation text, are kept for a year.

Actions
- Tomasz Lewandowski: update the draft and send it to Maya.

5 May 2025: Pilot review
Present: Maya Okafor, Daniel Reyes, Priya Nair, Tomasz Lewandowski, Hannah Frost.

Findings
- The four pilot users sent 1,386 prompts.
- There were four incidents during the pilot, INC-001 to INC-004. One was serious enough to be level S2: INC-003, where a client draft was pasted into the wrong project workspace.
- After INC-003 the team decided to show the workspace name as a banner at the top of every chat. Priya Nair made the change on 24 April.
- The satisfaction score from the survey was 4.3 out of 5.
- The 95th percentile response time is now 2.8 seconds, inside the target of 3 seconds.

Decisions
- Roll out to all 12 staff on 12 May.
- Everyone must attend a 45 minute training session first. Sessions are on 5 May and 6 May.
