## Goal
Add the other half of retrieval, keyword search, and a way to merge it with dense retrieval: `geekbot search --mode lexical` and `--mode hybrid` (PLAN.md §11 row S2.2; the ablation's rung R6).

A little context. Dense retrieval matches meaning and misses the exact rare token (an error code, a name); BM25 matches words and misses the synonym. To use both, their ranked lists must be merged, and adding the scores does not work: a cosine similarity lives in [-1, 1] and a BM25 score in the tens. **Reciprocal Rank Fusion** keeps only the ranks: a chunk's score is the sum of `1 / (60 + rank)` over the lists it is in, so a chunk near the top of both wins, one at the top of a single list still counts, and nothing depends on the scale of the original scores.

**Changes during the build:** the first fusion tests passed, then a review of the sort key found a real weakness: sums of reciprocals that are equal on paper can differ in the last bits when added in a different order, so a tie could be broken by floating-point noise and the ranking could depend on the order of the lists. The sort now rounds the score to 12 places before applying the tie-breakers, and a test with three-term sums checks it.
