## Goal
The measuring tape for everything that follows: retrieval metrics (nDCG, Recall, MRR) and the statistics that say whether a gap between two systems is real (paired bootstrap interval, permutation test, Holm correction), as the plan fixes them in §9 (PLAN.md §11 row S2.5).

A little context. A retrieval score averaged over a few hundred questions is a noisy number; two systems scored on another few hundred questions would give a different gap. So the plan never reports a gap alone: first the difference, then its 95% confidence interval, then a p-value, then a correction for having asked many questions of the same data. Doing this on the per-question *differences* (a paired design) removes the variation between easy and hard questions, which hits both systems alike. And a question with no relevant document at all has no recall: it must be left out of an average, not counted as zero, or a system that correctly abstains would be punished.
