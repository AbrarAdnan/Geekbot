## Goal
Re-score the best candidates with a model that reads the question and a passage together, and measure what that costs in time at each depth (PLAN.md §11 row S2.3; the ablation's rung R7): `geekbot search --mode hybrid_rerank` and `geekbot rerank-timing`.

A little context. Dense and BM25 look at the question and a chunk *separately*, and compare afterwards; that is fast (the chunk side is computed once, at ingest) and loses detail. A **cross-encoder** reads the question and one passage in a single pass and says how well the passage answers it: much more accurate, far too slow to run over every chunk. So it runs only on the few dozen candidates the fast retrievers found, a funnel that is wide and cheap first, narrow and careful last. On the CPU its time grows with the number of candidates (the depth), and the plan gives retrieval plus reranking 400 ms at p95, so the depth has to be measured, not guessed.
